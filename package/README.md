# MountedArchery

Draw, aim, loose. Still in the saddle. Bows, crossbows, thrown spears and the staffs that shoot something all work while
you ride, with no jumping off first. Lox, Asksvin, Moose, and any other saddled animal a mod gives you. Nothing to
install on the server.

## How to Use

1. Get on your animal.
2. Ready your bow, crossbow, spear or staff and shoot. You turn in the saddle to face your aim, fire, and settle back.
3. Keep steering. The reins never leave your hands.

Swinging a sword, jumping, dodging or pressing Use still gets you off, like in the unmodded game.

## Same Rules as the Game

Shots fly exactly as they do on foot: same aim, same arrows, same stamina. Your own animal can't be hit by your shots
unless PvP is on. Your friends see you fire from the saddle and settle back.

## Multiplayer

Only you need the mod. Nothing new is sent to anyone: friends without it see the game's own riding and shooting
animations.

## Compatibility

- Built and tested for Valheim 1.0 (Deep North), on Linux; no OS-specific code.
- It only changes what happens when you shoot from a saddle, so it shouldn't clash with other mods, except ones that
  also change riding or mounted combat (OdinHorse has its own mounted combat: turn one of them off).

## Uninstalling

Nothing is saved, so just remove it.
