# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- First release: shoot bows, crossbows, thrown spears, bombs and projectile staffs from the saddle of any rideable
  animal without being thrown off; you turn to face your aim for the shot.
- Your gallop carries into the shot: a spear or bomb thrown ahead flies ahead, one thrown back falls shorter.
- Enemies' health bars show while you ride, and riding over your thrown spear or loot picks it up.
